Incident Response Procedure (sample public-services procedure, synthetic)

Any security incident must be reported to the Security Operations Center within one hour of discovery.

Incidents are classified into four severity levels, from Sev1 (critical) to Sev4 (low).

For Sev1 incidents an incident commander must be assigned within 15 minutes of the report.

Only the Communications Office may issue public statements about an incident.

Affected citizens must be notified within 72 hours of a confirmed personal data breach.

Evidence such as disk images and logs must be preserved in write-once storage.

A post-incident review must be held within five business days of resolution.

The on-call rotation changes every Monday at 09:00.
