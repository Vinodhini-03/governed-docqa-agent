Remote Work Policy (sample public-services policy, synthetic)

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Remote work requests must be submitted at least five working days in advance.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, during which employees must be reachable by chat or phone.

Each remote employee receives a one-time home office stipend of USD 400. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of purchase.

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and connect through the corporate VPN when accessing internal systems.

Employees who wish to work from a different country need written approval from both HR and Legal before travelling.

Any damage to company equipment must be reported to IT within 48 hours of discovery.

Performance of remote employees is assessed on outcomes delivered, not on hours spent online.
