Data Retention Guideline (sample public-services guideline, synthetic)

Financial records are retained for seven years from the end of the fiscal year in which they were created.

Employee personnel files are retained for five years after the employee separates from the organisation.

Routine email is retained for three years and is then permanently deleted.

Access logs for internal systems are retained for 12 months.

All backups are encrypted with AES-256 and stored in a separate geographic region from the primary data.

Data classified as Restricted requires approval from the Data Protection Officer before it is shared externally.

Deletion requests from citizens must be answered within 30 days of receipt.

Exceptions to retention periods are granted only by the Records Committee, and only for an active legal hold.
